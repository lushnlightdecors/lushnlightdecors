Our Story

A Passion That Grew Into LushnLight

LushnLight began with a shared passion for creating meaningful celebrations. What started with an appreciation for decor and design grew into something bigger: a desire to help people bring their special moments to life with beauty, creativity and care.

For us, decor is not simply about placing flowers, balloons or a backdrop in a room. It is about understanding how a celebration should feel, then using colour, texture, lighting, proportion and thoughtful details to create that feeling.

One side of our partnership naturally sees the possibilities—the colours that could work together, the shape a backdrop could take, or the detail that could make an ordinary corner feel special. The other asks: How can we build it? How can we make it work beautifully in the real space? That combination became the foundation of LushnLight Decors.

Creative Designer & Creative Lead

Prabhdeep Kaur

Prabhdeep is the creative mind behind LushnLight. Her focus is bringing fresh ideas, unique concepts and thoughtful design details to every celebration.

From colour combinations and floral styling to balloon palettes, backdrops, textures and the overall balance of a space, she explores how individual elements can come together as one complete design. Her goal is not simply to repeat what has already been done, but to keep discovering ways to make each celebration feel personal, elegant and memorable.

Her curiosity drives the creative side of LushnLight—continually exploring new possibilities while keeping the client's vision at the centre.

Planning, Build & Execution

Inderjeet Singh

Inderjeet takes the creative direction and works out how to bring it into the real world. His role is where an idea becomes something that can actually be planned, built, transported, installed and experienced.

From figuring out how different elements come together to adapting and building pieces, coordinating the practical details and overseeing the final setup, his focus is on turning a concept into a finished environment while preserving the original creative intent.

When Prabhdeep sees what a space could become, Inderjeet focuses on how to make that vision possible. It is a hands-on partnership where imagination and problem-solving continually meet.

Always Evolving

We Never Want Creativity to Stand Still.

Celebrations change. Design changes. New materials, colour combinations, styling approaches and ideas constantly create new possibilities—and we believe a creative business should evolve with them.

That doesn't mean blindly chasing every trend. It means staying curious. We explore new concepts, learn from every setup and look for ways to make the next design more thoughtful, more practical and more visually engaging.

Sometimes that means experimenting with a new palette. Sometimes it is finding a better way to construct a backdrop, rethinking floral placement, introducing a different texture or adapting an inspiration to work beautifully within a client's actual venue.

We are comfortable changing an idea when changing it makes the result better.

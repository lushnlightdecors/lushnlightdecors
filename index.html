About us

Creating Unforgettable Moments

At LushnLight Decors, we specialize in floral, balloon and lighting decor for weddings, birthdays, baby showers, Aqiqah and other special celebrations. We focus on thoughtful details, elegant styling and a smooth experience from your first inquiry through your event setup.

Every celebration is different, so we work with your theme and vision to create a setup that feels personal to you.

Have a celebration coming up?

Tell us your event date, location, theme, colours and the type of setup you have in mind. We’ll help you explore the right decor.

Creating Unforgettable Moments

At Lush N Light Decors, we specialize in floral, balloon and lighting decor for weddings, birthdays, baby showers, Aqiqah and other special celebrations. We focus on thoughtful details, elegant styling and a smooth experience from your first inquiry through your event setup.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Toronto and communities across the Greater Toronto Area. Share your event location with us and we’ll confirm availability and delivery details.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking early gives you the best selection and planning time, especially for weekends and popular event dates. Contact us as soon as you know your date.

Do you deliver and set up the decor?

Yes. Delivery and setup can be included based on your package and event location. Your quote will clearly outline the included services.

Can you match my theme or colours?

Absolutely. Share your colours, theme and inspiration photos in the quote form and we can discuss a setup that fits your celebration.

Can I send an inspiration photo?

Yes. You can upload an inspiration image or share a link directly through our free quote form.
